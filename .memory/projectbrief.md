# Project Brief — Landing Page Gereja Baitani

## Project Overview
Modern, brutalist, and sacred Avant-Garde landing page for Gereja Baitani, taking aesthetic inspiration from GMS Church (`https://gms.church/`) while preserving unique community identity.

## Architecture
- Hybrid Single-Page + Multi-Page Architecture:
  - Homepage (`/`) operates as a single-page with smooth scrollspy navigation.
  - 8 Dedicated individual routes (`/tentang-kami`, `/acara`, `/lokasi`, `/connect-group`, `/gembala`, `/sorotan`, `/persembahan`, `/koneksi`).
- Palette: Deep Obsidian (`#050505`), Vault Charcoal (`#111111`), Sacred Gold (`#D4AF37`), Tinted Warm Accents (`#E5C07B`, `#F3E5AB`). Zero AI gradients or blue/purple slop.
- Typography: Cinzel (Display Serif) + Plus Jakarta Sans (Body Sans).
